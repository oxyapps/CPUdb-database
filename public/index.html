CPUdb data

This host serves the CPUdb processor database: the specifications of released CPUs and SoCs that the CPUdb app and website use.

  • manifest.json: the current dataset, its version and SHA-256.
  • cpus-<version>.json.gz: the dataset itself (gzipped JSON).

The data is licensed under the GNU GPL v3.0. The format, the sources and how to report a correction are on GitHub.
